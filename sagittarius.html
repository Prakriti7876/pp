Sagittarius ♐️: (November 22 – December 21)

Element: Fire 🔥

Ruling Planet: Jupiter

Symbol: Archer 🏹

Greatest Compatibility: Aries, Leo, Aquarius

Toxic Pairing: Cancer, Virgo, Scoprio

Lucky Number: 3, 5, 7, 21, 33

💖 Personality

💙 Traits

Sagittarius, the adventurous and optimistic fire sign, is known for traits like being free-spirited, intellectual, and having a great sense of humor, but can also be blunt and impatient.

🌸 Strengths

  • adventurous Spirit
  • Honest to the core
  • Independent
  • Wise
  • Optimistic & High-Vibe

💔 Weaknesses

  • Communication Issues
  • Restless and Impulsive
  • Emtionally Detached

👍🏻 Sagittarius likes:

Adventure & exploration, philosophy & deep conversation, freedom & independence, humor, Challenges & Risk

👎🏻 Sagittarius dislikes:

Clingy or Controlling Behavior, boring routines, emotional drama, lies, judgmental, close-mined people

💘 Love & Relationships

When it comes to love, Sagittarius doesn’t follow the rules. They burn them, charm you mid-chaos, and disappear into the night sky with your heart in their pocket. This sign is a wild-hearted wanderer in love—passionate, curious, and brutally honest... but sometimes elusive.“Love me with no leash, or don’t love me at all.”

👛 Career & Money

Sagittarius in the career arena is like a firework in a library: brilliant, loud, a little chaotic, but unforgettable, They’re not built for boring routines or gray office cubicles—they crave meaning, movement, and a purpose bigger than themselves. Think of them as the philosopher-warrior of the zodiac: always searching, always pushing boundaries, and lowkey accidentally starting revolutions at work
