Aries ♈️:(Mar 21 – Apr 19)

Element: Fire 🔥

Ruling Planet: Mars

Symbol: The Ram 🐏

Greatest Compatibility: Leo, Sagittarius, Gemini

Toxic Pairing: Cancer, Capricorn, Virgo

Lucky Number: 1, 6, 9

💖 Personality

💙 Traits

Aries is bold, daring, and full of fiery energy. They're natural leaders who light up every room with confidence and sparkle. Expect lots of excitement and spontaneous adventures with this sign!

🌸 Strengths

  • Fearless
  • Ambitious
  • Energetic
  • Confident
  • Playful

💔 Weaknesses

  • Impulsive
  • Hot-headed
  • Competitive (a *bit* too much)

👍🏻 Aries likes:

being in charge, honesty, enjoys trying new things, trips.

👎🏻 Aries dislike:

being told what to do, being taken for granted, waiting.

💘 Love & Relationships

In love, Aries is passionate and flirty. They crave chemistry, fun, and someone who can keep up with their fiery heart. They give love 110%!

👛 Career & Money

Ambition is their middle name. Aries shine in leadership roles and creative paths. They're go-getters with a spark that inspires everyone around them.

📖 In a story

Courage is not just about rushing in. It’s about knowing when to wait, when to listen, and when to grow.
