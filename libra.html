Ruling Planet: Venus

Symbol:The scales

💖 Personality

💙 Traits

“The scale is never neutral. It only waits until you're vulnerable enough to be judged.” “They’ll smile as they weigh your soul, speak gently while deciding your fate, and when the scales tip... you won’t see the blade until it’s too late.”

🌸 Strengths

  • Diplomatic
  • Charming
  • Good at seeing multiple perspectives
  • Values peace and harmony
  • Fair minded

💔 Weaknesses

  • Indecisive
  • Avoidant
  • Overly idealistic
  • Passive-aggressive tendencies

👍🏻 Libra likes:

Beauty, peaceful enviroments, music, art, poetry, strong partnership

👎🏻 Libra dislikes:

Arguments and conflict, Ugly or chaotic spaces, Being alone for too long, Harsh criticism, Decisions with no clear “right” answer

💘 Love & Relationships

Ruled by Venus, Libra lives for love and deep emotional connection.They want things to feel mutual, fair, and equal. No one-sided love stories.“To love a Libra is to step into a mirror — where beauty hides the judgment, and sweetness is the silence before the scream.”

“Where beauty meets balance, Libra builds a career that connects, calms, and captivates.” “They say justice wears a blindfold. But Libra sees the truth — and it’s hideous.”
